01

定位 / Purpose

MEME.LAB 是离线 Meme 市场博弈模拟游戏。玩家使用虚拟 BNB 发行 10 亿枚代币，观察散户、交易员、KOL、巨鲸和新闻事件如何通过实际订单形成价格。所有交易与角色皆为模拟，不涉及真实钱包、链上代币或收益承诺。

MEME.LAB is an offline meme-market strategy game. Players launch a simulated token with a fixed one-billion supply and use virtual BNB. Prices emerge from simulated orders—not from a predetermined chart. No real assets, wallets or investment returns are involved.

02

玩家目标 / Gameplay

玩家持有 100 枚虚拟 BNB（与 AI 钱包四档资金完全独立），自由买卖、调配 LP、开关做市。可以暂停、单回合推进或使用最高 5× 加速；无限回合，随时暂停。市场没有预设必须上涨或毕业的结局。

Start with 100 simulated BNB (player-only bankroll); AI traders use separate capped wallets denominated in BNB at the fixed reference rate. Trade, manage LP, enable market-making, pause, step forward or accelerate. The game runs for unlimited rounds at up to 5× speed. No guaranteed rally or graduation.

03

发射与供给 / Launch and Supply

创世总发行量固定为 1,000,000,000 枚，经过销毁后未销毁供应量会下降。发行时按选择的比例将代币存入模拟曲线库存，其余进入协议锁仓，玩家初始不持有可流通代币。所有钱包、流动性池、协议锁仓、回购金库持仓及累计销毁合计始终等于初始发行量。完全稀释估值 模拟市值 = 当前 BNB/币价格 × 当前未销毁供应量，美元数字只是按静态参考汇率换算。

Exactly 1,000,000,000 tokens are simulated. The launch allocation splits supply between curve inventory and protocol-locked inventory. The player starts with no free liquid tokens. Wallet balances, pool inventory and locked tokens must sum to the supply. Fully diluted valuation (FDV) equals token price in BNB times the current unburned supply. USD estimates use an illustrative fixed conversion.

04

联合曲线 / Bonding Curve

使用简化的常数乘积虚拟储备：x × y = k。买入向 BNB 储备投入资金、提取曲线中的代币；卖出反之。虚拟储备只用于定价，曲线阶段真实可兑付 BNB 不得超过实际净流入。池子采用 0.3% 模拟兑换手续费；另外，发行者可以设置 0–10% 买入税与卖出税。税款由实际虚拟 BNB 交易支付并存入项目金库。

A simplified virtual-reserve constant-product bonding curve uses x × y = k. Buying adds BNB and removes tokens from inventory; selling reverses that trade. Virtual reserves set prices but redemption is capped by actual net BNB collected. An illustrative 0.3% pool fee applies separately from configurable 0–10% buy and sell taxes in BNB. Collected fees enter the token treasury.

05

毕业机制 / DEX Graduation

本游戏采用原创教学化简条件：曲线库存至少售出 80%，且曲线实际净储备达到约 55.4 BNB，才执行 DEX 毕业。迁移时把可用 BNB 和按迁移价格匹配的代币存入恒定乘积池，协议 LP 锁定，其余未分配代币进入锁仓。这不是 Flap 官方智能合约的逐参数复刻。

The original educational graduation rule requires at least 80% of curve inventory sold and roughly 55.4 BNB net backing. Graduation places BNB and matching token inventory into a constant-product DEX pool, locks protocol LP and sequesters leftover tokens. This is not a parameter-exact reproduction of Flap contracts.

06

市场行为 / Market Agents

1,800 个独立模拟散户与交易员，分散户 $1K、中户 $10K、大户 $100K、超级大户 $200K 四档初始资金（按固定参考汇率折合 BNB），加上虚构 KOL、巨鲸及玩家。每个账户拥有有限 BNB、代币库存、平均成本及已实现盈亏。行情动量、注意力和情绪提高或降低买入倾向；浮盈可能触发止盈，浮亏可能导致恐慌卖出。事件只是改变参与者决策与订单流，并不直接指定下一根 K 线。

The market includes 1,800 independent retail and trader accounts plus fictional KOLs and whales. Four simulated capital tiers ($1K, $10K, $100K and $200K USDT-equivalent) govern their starting BNB balances. Each tracks finite BNB, token balances, average cost and realized P&L. Momentum, attention and sentiment influence decisions; profits prompt taking gains while losses can trigger panic selling. Events influence orders, not scripted future candles.

07

行情、榜单与事件 / Charts

每回合生成一根 OHLC K 线和成交量，持续运行且不再在第 444 回合强制结算；为控制浏览器性能，图表只保留最近 900 根 K 线。KOL 的公开提及、买卖和其他重要事件会在图上留下头像或标记；最近卖方记录包含原始持仓成本与已实现收益。持仓表只展示前 100 名，并显示 U 计价的买入均价与 K/M 格式的 USDT 持仓市值。系统有多种利好、利空和中性事件，但不保证每局都触发相同剧情。

Each round creates an OHLC candle and volume bar. Fictional KOL mentions and trades, as well as key events, receive chart markers. Recent sales list cost basis and realized results. The holdings leaderboard displays only the top 100 wallets. Event timing depends on the seeded simulation.

08

玩家做市与 LP / Market Making

发射期可以主动交易并制定偏离参考价的自动买卖规则；毕业后还可以按池内比例添加与撤回双边 LP。所有订单从玩家钱包扣除真实的模拟资产余额，可能导致滑点、库存枯竭或亏损。游戏内做市不是现实金融市场服务。

Trade manually or use a price-band strategy; after graduation provide and withdraw paired-token LP. Every order deducts simulated wallet assets. Slippage, inventory depletion and losses are possible. No real-world market-making service is provided.

09

时间、存档与公平 / Saves

每回合代表 1 分钟虚拟市场时间，不等于现实世界的实时成交数据。点击存档或每 15 回合自动存档，数据保存在当前浏览器 localStorage；读档可继续。更换浏览器、清除站点数据或无痕模式可能丢档。同样的随机种子主要用于可重复实验，玩家动作会改变后续结果。

One round represents one in-game minute, not a live market minute. Manual saves and autosaves every 15 rounds use browser localStorage; clearing site data or using another device may erase progress. Seeds enable experiments, but user actions change outcomes.

V8.2.1 · FDV 与销毁对账 / Supply and Valuation Audit

全站市值用同一回合价格快照。销毁后 FDV = 当前 U 单价 ×（1,000,000,000 − 永久销毁量）。回购持仓尚未销毁，仍计入未销毁供应量。流通供应/估值另外扣除协议锁仓与金库回购持仓。

例如单价 0.000914508 U、永久销毁 35.71M 枚时：原始估值约 $914,508；销毁等值约 $32,657；销毁后 FDV 约 $881,851。分开拍摄不同回合会出现数字不匹配，新对账面板的回合快照提供逐项核验。

“用于销毁 99%”是后续税收回购预算的分配，不是立即烧毁 99% 的总供应。每 8 回合最多动用 1 虚拟 BNB 回购；税率与预算比例修改不追溯历史。回购影响价格，单纯销毁不直接改变 AMM 池价格。

Unburned FDV = unit price × (genesis supply − burned tokens). Treasury buyback-held tokens remain part of unburned supply. A separately shown estimated circulating value excludes treasury-held and protocol-locked inventory. Every audit uses a single round snapshot; tax allocation changes apply prospectively.

11 · 税收、销毁与回购 / Configurable Tokenomics

发行时及游戏中，玩家均可修改买税与卖税（各 0–10%，默认 2% / 2%），以及税收用于销毁与用于回购持仓的比例（两者总和不得超过 100%，默认 50% + 30%）。虚拟 BNB 税款存入项目金库，每 8 回合最多使用 1 BNB 进行实际模拟回购：销毁部分永久退出流通量，回购部分保留在金库钱包，未分配税款留在金库。税收比例修改仅对后续成交生效，不会篡改历史订单。

Players can set 0–10% buy/sell taxes and allocate collected BNB tax revenue between simulated buy-and-burn and treasury buybacks. Treasury purchases are real trades against the simulated curve/AMM pool, not direct price changes. The original one-billion genesis supply remains conserved when burned tokens are included in the accounting audit.

12 · 排行榜 / Rankings

持仓榜继续展示前 100 名交易钱包。新增市场交易员收益率排名及同浏览器历史战绩榜。历史积分采用风险调整的收益率及回合数，至少运行 30 回合才可登记。本阶段榜单仅存储在玩家自己的浏览器，不是全球在线排行榜，也不能防止本地修改数据。跨玩家的真实联机排名需要后端身份认证与服务端校验。

The in-session wallet ROI table and browser-local historical score list are for personal comparison only. A global leaderboard requires a trusted backend and is not implemented in this static release.
